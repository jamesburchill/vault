# Give Your MCP Service a Predictable Address

If you’re making an MCP service available for your business, consider giving it a predictable address:

**mcp.yourdomain.tld**

I use **mcp.jamesburchill.com**. It’s easy to recognize, easy to share, and lets me change the infrastructure behind it without changing the address people use.

This is a naming convention I’d like to see more businesses adopt. It won’t make AI assistants automatically discover or connect to your service, but it gives people a sensible place to look.

Publish your connection instructions on your website, too. Make it easy.
