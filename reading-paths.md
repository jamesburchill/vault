# Reading Paths

Start with the problem or idea that interests you. These are selected routes through the Vault, not a required reading order.

- [Building with AI](#building-with-ai)
- [Architecture and Operations](#architecture-and-operations)
- [Business and Product](#business-and-product)
- [Writing and Judgment](#writing-and-judgment)

<h2 id="building-with-ai">Building with AI</h2>

For people turning ideas into working software and deciding how much authority to give their tools.

1. [AI Can Generate Software. Reality Still Gets A Vote.](/content/2026/05/ai-can-generate-software-reality-still-gets-a-vote/) — Start with the gap between a working demonstration and a system ready for use.
2. [Runtime Isolation Is Not Governance](/content/2026/05/runtime-isolation-is-not-governance/) — Separate the ability to execute an action from permission to take it.
3. [Local-First AI: Put the Control Plane Back Inside the Business](/content/2026/06/local-first-ai-control-plane/) — Explore model-assisted routing with independently enforced permissions and budgets.

<h2 id="architecture-and-operations">Architecture and Operations</h2>

For technical leaders and builders concerned with maintenance, recovery, and the consequences of design decisions.

1. [Why Deployment Should Be Boring](/content/2026/07/why-deployment-should-be-boring/) — Begin the seven-part Inside the Architecture series.
2. [Why Predictable Recovery Matters More Than Perfect Uptime](/content/2026/07/why-predictable-recovery-matters-more-than-perfect-uptime/) — Think about what happens after a failure.
3. [Why We Don’t Build Custom Operating Systems](/content/2026/07/why-we-dont-build-custom-operating-systems/) — Decide which engineering obligations are worth taking on.

<h2 id="business-and-product">Business and Product</h2>

For founders and operators connecting product decisions to useful outcomes for customers.

1. [Build for Recurring Usefulness and Earned Trust](/content/2025/05/painkillers-arent-enough-why-your-software-product-needs-to-solve-a-repeated-addictive-problem/) — Look for recurring value without making dependence the goal.
2. [Semantic Objects Come Before Semantic Indexes](/content/2026/06/semantic-objects-come-before-semantic-indexes/) — Understand what a business information system needs to preserve before choosing retrieval technology.
3. [By Inches](/content/2026/05/by-inches/) — Notice how reasonable exceptions can accumulate into an expensive change of direction.

<h2 id="writing-and-judgment">Writing and Judgment</h2>

For readers interested in how ideas are communicated, challenged, and revised.

1. [By Inches](/content/2026/05/by-inches/) — A personal account of recognizing drift before having a name for it.
2. [Writing for Search and AI Systems Without Chasing a New Acronym](/content/2025/05/the-evolution-of-search-from-keywords-to-context-and-the-rise-of-aiso/) — Publish useful evidence without promising visibility you cannot control.
3. [Governing Intelligent Systems](/governing-intelligent-systems/) — Explore the systems theme connecting these notes and its working vocabulary.

For a specific question, [search the Vault](/search/). To explore by publication date, [browse the archive](/content/).
