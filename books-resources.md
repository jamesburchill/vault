# Books & Resources

A few ways to read more deeply and put the ideas to work.

## The Blind Squirrel Method

A free illustrated fable about luck, skill and the stories we sell. Pip finds an acorn; before long, the wood is paying to learn his secrets. The book includes a checklist and worksheet for examining success claims.

[Read about the book and download the free PDF](https://jamesburchill.com/blind-squirrel-method/). No signup required.

## Learning Materials

- [Reading Paths](/reading-paths/) -- Selected writing on AI, architecture, business and judgement, with introductions to help you choose a starting point.
- [Governing Intelligent Systems](/governing-intelligent-systems/) -- A technical guide to drift, governance and the control surfaces of intelligent systems.

## Practical Tools

[Connect your AI assistant](https://jamesburchill.com/mcp/) to search my public writing, retrieve articles and find their sources. The connection guide explains what the service can do and how to use it.
