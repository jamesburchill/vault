# About the Vault

I’m James Burchill. For more than four decades, I have founded companies, led engineering teams, built software, written, taught, and worked with organizations navigating change.

Software engineering, publishing, marketing, teaching, and entrepreneurship all inform how I approach a problem. The Vault brings those perspectives together in essays, practical explanations, and working ideas.

## What You’ll Find Here

I write about building with AI, making architecture decisions, designing useful products, preserving context, and communicating ideas clearly. Some pieces explain a specific engineering choice. Others start with a business problem or a pattern I have noticed over time.

[Because Drift Happens™](https://becausedrifthappens.com/) connects much of this work. Plans, software, processes, and assumptions change. I’m interested in how we notice that movement and keep systems useful, understandable, and accountable.

These are field notes, not a claim that every question is settled. I distinguish proposals from established practice and revisit arguments as the evidence and my understanding develop. Original publication dates remain intact; substantive revisions carry a review note.

## The Work Behind the Writing

My work spans technical leadership, independent ventures, and selected private engagements. I currently serve as CTO at Tooljar while also developing my own products and working directly with other founders and organizations.

The public examples here illustrate parts of that work. Private engagements remain private.

For an overview of my work and engagements, visit [JamesBurchill.com](https://jamesburchill.com/). You can also find me on [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/jamesburchill/).

## A Durable, Inspectable Collection

The site is intentionally simple. Articles have stable addresses, and each offers its Markdown source and a provenance record. Feeds and machine-readable files are available in the footer for readers and tools that need them.

Provenance helps establish a page’s origin and integrity; readers still need to assess its argument and evidence. Publishing details are documented in the [repository README](https://github.com/jamesburchill/vault#readme).
